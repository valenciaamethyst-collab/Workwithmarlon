All prices are in USD. Final fees reflect the agreed scope. Payments accepted through PayPal, Payoneer, or bank transfer.

How do monthly plans work?

We start with a discovery conversation about your business and current setup. Then we agree on a written list of deliverables, quantities, deadlines, working hours, and a fixed monthly fee. Additional tasks or a larger workload receive a revised scope and quote.

How do revisions and approvals work?

Each deliverable includes two revision rounds, with one consolidated set of feedback per round. Corrections to agreed requirements are included. A new direction or additional deliverable is scoped separately. You review and approve content and important changes before publication or campaign sending.

When and how will we communicate?

I'm based in the Philippines, with overnight availability for U.S. and Canadian weekday collaboration. We agree on exact working hours and response expectations before starting. You'll receive daily written updates, with focused calls when a project or decision needs a conversation.

What about urgent work or a new website?

Rush and weekend requests are quoted separately and depend on availability. Full website builds and complex integrations receive their own scope and quote. We can discuss a one-off project during our introduction.
